title=LX Split Aim
description=[h1]LX Split Aim[/h1]
description=[i]Controller aim that works, for every local player[/i]
description=B42 made shooting with a gamepad nearly impossible. This gives it back - for all 4 split-screen players at once.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Shoot zombies on the ground[/b] - B42's controller aim cannot resolve height: a zombie lying on the floor, crawling, or one level up or down is effectively unshootable.
description=[*][b]Every player gets their own aim[/b] - The mod this forks kept aim state in global flags, so in split-screen one player kept overwriting the other's.
description=[*][b]B41-style lock-on[/b] - A configurable cone picks the zombie closest to where you are pushing the stick, and the reticle snaps and then tracks it as it moves. Optional head targeting.
description=[*][b]Soft-snap when nothing is locked[/b] - Separate horizontal, vertical and head strengths, hitbox zone sizes, pull-up easing to reach the head, recenter resistance and a stick response curve - all…
description=[*][b]Stays out of the way[/b] - Aim assist switches off automatically while that player has a radial menu, inventory, map or any UI holding their controller - and for the keyboard-and-mouse…
description=[/list]
description=[b]Requires:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url]
description=[b]Load it AFTER[/b] ZombieBuddy.
description=[b]Do not enable together with[/b] Controller Aim Assist (B42).
description=[b]!![/b] First launch: ZombieBuddy asks you to approve this mod's JAR by its SHA-256 before loading it. That is normal - tick remember so it stops asking.
description=[b]Install:[/b] Subscribe, then enable it under Mods in the main menu. In split-screen, enabling it once covers every player.
description=[b]Options:[/b] Options → MODS → [b]LX Split Aim[/b]. Master switch, lock-on (cone, speed, floors up/down, head targeting, marker), soft-snap strengths, hitbox zones, recenter resistance and stick curve.
